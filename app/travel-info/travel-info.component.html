Getting to Brown Mountain Beach Resort — Lenoir, NC

Nearest Airports

  • Charlotte Douglas International (CLT) – ~1.5 hrs drive
  • Concord-Padgett Regional Airport (USA) – ~1hr 45 mins drive
  • Asheville Regional Airport (AVL) – ~1 hr 45 mins drive
  • Piedmont Triad International Airport (PTI) ~2 hrs drive

✈️ Charlotte is the most convenient for most guests

🚗 Concord is a smaller airport but a great option if your local airport offers flights through Allegiant or Avelo.

We recommend booking your flights as soon as possible, as prices can fluctuate.

Rental cars are available at all nearby airports. Free parking is available on-site at the resort.

If you’re staying on-site, no transportation is needed once you arrive.

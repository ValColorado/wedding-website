🌲 Things To Do During Our Wedding Weekend

While we encourage you to immerse in the beauty of North Carolina, the heart of the weekend is right at the resort — relaxing by the river, grilling, sipping drinks, and making memories with friends and family. 🍹🔥

But, in case you'd like to take a little adventure, here are a few lovely spots we recommend exploring around NC. 🌄

🥾 Nature & Trails

  • Harper Creek Falls – A moderate hike with breathtaking views
  • Linville Gorge & Falls – Stunning cliffs & classic NC scenery

☕ Local Cafes & Bites

  • Blue Moose Coffee Lodge – Cozy coffee + mountain views
  • Dooley’s Grill & Tavern – Amazing burgers & local beers in downtown Lenoir
  • Bella Torte Bakery – Sweet treats + southern charm

🏘️ Local Gems

  • Lenoir Downtown – Antique shops, murals & slow southern strolls
  • Hickory Furniture Mart – For anyone who loves home decor
  • Moses Cone Manor – A historic home with art & beautiful grounds

🏞 Asheville

Asheville is a funky mountain town about 1.5 hours west of the venue, known for its art scene, breweries, and stunning views of the Blue Ridge Parkway.

  • Stroll through the River Arts District
  • Hike at Craggy Gardens or Pis

Q: What should I wear for hanging out at the resort?

  • Swimwear: Perfect for a refreshing dip in the mountain river.
  • Comfortable Footwear: Ideal for exploring trails or strolling along the creek.
  • Casual Attire: Lightweight and breathable clothing for daytime activities.
  • Layers: Evenings can be cool, so a light jacket or sweater is recommended.
  • Sun Protection: Hats, sunglasses, and sunscreen to shield from the sun.

Whether you're tubing down the river, enjoying a meal at the café, or simply relaxing by the fire pit, comfort is key. Embrace the rustic charm and natural beauty of the resort with attire that suits the serene mountain setting.

A: Nope! The Venue is in the Pisgah National Forest, where cell phone service does not reach. We love this! We want to invite you to put up ALL your devices and be present with our friends and family. If needed, we do have WiFi up at our Rock Pavilion and main deck area.
